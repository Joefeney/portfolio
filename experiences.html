e-RoomReserve

Capstone and IoT project · In progress

Development of a Smart Room Reservation and Occupancy Monitoring System Using BLE Beacon Technology for St. Dominic College of Asia

A room reservation web app with Firebase Authentication and Firestore, plus reservation document uploads using Supabase Storage.

The app also includes Next.js API routes, PDF and Excel exports, VADER-based sentiment analysis, and Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) room-status features.
